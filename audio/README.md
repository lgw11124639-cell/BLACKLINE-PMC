# Local audio samples

Licenses and source links: [credits.html](credits.html). Runtime does not contact any third-party audio site. Ten AAC/M4A samples total approximately 350 KB, loaded only after the audio control is pressed. Chromium and Safari support AAC in MP4.

Gunshots use one isolated take per file from The Free Firearm Sound Library (CC0). Each original contains multiple takes; extracted offsets (seconds) are rifle-a 0.68, rifle-b 5.62 (D_32P), rifle-distant 0.56 (D_24P), pistol 1.06 (X_31P), precision 0.75 (W_24P), ak 0.59 (C_28P). Tails fade out before the next take. Files are mono 32 kHz AAC 80 kbps, high-passed at 40 Hz, loudness normalized to -20 LUFS / -3 dBTP. Engine loops are -23 LUFS / -5 dBTP and have 100 ms boundary crossfades. Originals are not bundled.

Samples represent weapon families, not authentic recordings of every in-game weapon model. A suppressor is represented by filtering and level reduction; supersonic ammunition retains an audible attack. Short room/ridge impulses, propagation delay and stereo panning are presentation effects and do not change simulated hearing or AI.
